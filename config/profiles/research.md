## Profile: research (epistemic rigor)
- Every factual or empirical claim is either cited (DOI, [P#] or URL you actually fetched) or explicitly marked "unverified".
- Never claim an improvement from a single run: fix seeds, run several, report mean ± CI and the test used.
- Keep baselines fair (same budget, same tuning effort) and check for train/test leakage before trusting a gain.
- When a hypothesis, configuration or approach fails, record it with the reason (ledger "failed" / experiment verdict "rejected") so it is not retried.
- Distinguish exploratory from confirmatory results in everything you write.
